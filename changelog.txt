Revision 2026.11.06:

First AI Suite bundle. One download, four mod folders.

- LIES 8.57.32 (f9f6663) - enemy AI: Reaction Level rework with a new HIGH
  value, taser settle-check crash fix (8.57.31)
- Monkeepers 87.4 (cbb0aad) - bot bag logistics
- Iter 205.3 (44f6f8d) - navigation: opaque-userdata shape audit, no gameplay
  change
- Keepers 239.3 (6601444) - bot orders

Load order (priority descending): LIES 100, Monkeepers 99, Iter 40,
Keepers 10. Tested with Useful Bots 2.6.3 + HopLib 2.1.2; The Fixes optional.

The bundle is assembled from the four mod repositories at pinned commits; it
contains no merged code. Per-mod update feeds are unchanged.

Player notes: download one zip, extract the four folders into mods\, and you
get the full AI stack that used to need four separate installs. Enemy AI,
navigation, bot orders and bot bag carrying all behave exactly as in the
individual releases; Useful Bots still works alongside them. Two things worth
knowing if you are coming from the individual 8.57.30 / 205.2 builds: LIES
Reaction Level gained a HIGH setting and the menu values moved, so a profile
that had ULTRA-VIOLENCE selected will show HIGH until you pick ULTRA again;
and the 8.57.31 fix removes a taser-related crash, so Door Watching can stay
on. Iter 205.3 changes no gameplay.
